# Adversarial review

Use an independent critic when a material assumption, costly decision, or unresolved alternative warrants challenge. Select relevant lenses: simplicity, assumptions, deferral, the runner-up's strongest case, or missing failure modes. No minimum critic or finding count applies.

Give each critic the original brief, accepted constraints, candidate artifact, source evidence, and read access needed to check it. Exclude the author's deliberation when the harness supports a fresh context. Inherit the host model; schedule independent work within available slots. Zero substantiated findings is a valid result.

## Evidence and disposition

Merge findings only when their cause and consequence match. For each finding, record a correction, a refutation supported by evidence, or an unresolved risk with its effect on the decision. An author's confidence score cannot establish that a rebuttal is correct.

Where a consequential dispute remains, give a fresh reviewer the finding, source evidence, and rebuttal. The reviewer may confirm, refute, or identify missing evidence. Do not force certainty or decide by votes. Run another targeted pass only when it can resolve a specific remaining question; otherwise surface the uncertainty.

## Fallback and completion

Under economy mode or when independent review is unavailable, perform the relevant self-check directly. Describe this as a shared-context self-check, not independent verification. It does not remove the need to investigate material uncertainty.

Complete when the decision has adequate evidence and each material finding is addressed or explicitly remains a risk or user-owned question. A planning-only request ends with the artifact. An existing implementation mandate continues with the chosen workflow and accepted authorization.

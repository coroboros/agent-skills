# Spatial-organic — tier 1

**Voice.** Depth is physical, never decorated: z-axis layering instead of drop shadows, soft `clip-path` curves where a rectangle would snap, procedural animated texture (Canvas or WebGL noise, never PNG grain), and native browser APIs — View Transitions, Scroll-Driven Animations, WebGPU — driving what used to demand heavy JS. The palette is earthy and muted, found in nature, never neon and never corporate blue. The organizing law is ONE persistent spatial substrate — a living WebGL ground, an ambient OKLCH orb field, or procedural noise — carried hero to footer under a single reversible motion vocabulary (organic clip-path geometry, a variable-font axis scroll-morph, a named idle band). Cohesion lives in that substrate and one easing table; variety lives in the per-class mechanism.

**Register licence.** The register permits an entire page rendered as one real-time scene: Igloo Inc takes Site of the Year 2024 on a Three.js world with a 220-character `<body>` and zero text nodes, its Animations/Transitions sub-score 9.60. It equally permits zero WebGL — Cyd Stumpel takes SOTD 2025-03-09 at 7.22 (Developer Award 7.74) on native View Transitions and Scroll-Driven Animations alone, with periwinkle clip-path geometry as the substrate. Restraint binds on the palette, on glass contrast (4.5:1 or the glass is a defect), and on amplitude: ONE dominant peak plus an optional quiet second at the footer, and the dominant peak is free to land late. Restraint never binds the substrate. Silence is the sin here, not spectacle: a section that goes flat, rectangular and pointer-dead is "no depth", the archetype's defining defect.

**Anti-signals — these disqualify.**
- A pale pastel tint sweeping the fill on hover. Real fills are committed same-family tokens at 80–100%; a tint with no geometry move fails the pale-hover gate.
- A contrasting accent `border-bottom` under the nav on scroll. Winners carry a same-family hairline, a borderless solid bar, or a diegetic path instead of a bar.
- One universal hover copied across buttons, links, figures and rows — cohesion faked by repetition instead of earned from a shared substrate.
- A circle-follower cursor with magnetic-everything, reached for before any content decision.
- An undesigned full-screen spinner, or a bare `0–100%` gate that hard-cuts into the page.
- A blanket 40px fade-up on every section, with "spatial" resting on `backdrop-filter` alone; static PNG grain standing in for procedural texture; motionless SVG blobs standing in for organic form.
- Glass surfaces under 4.5:1 text contrast — the `award-winning-websites-2025-2030` reference article (`github.com/coroboros/research`) flags this line for it explicitly (§9.4).
- A bento or card-grid fold, a fat sitemap footer, a filled fold CTA, stock imagery, neon or corporate blue.

**Macrostructures it runs.**
- `engine-world` — the engine is the page: one continuous real-time scene, shader seams for section boundaries, the climax late and interactive. Route here when a real 3D world exists AND a WebGL path is committed. (Igloo Inc, winner-verified)
- `studio-reel` — the warm-organic register with no WebGL at all: marquee hero, role band, morph-tile work grid, contact-first close, the peak at the fold. Route here for a solo or studio brand with no WebGL path — an ambient orb field is the substrate instead of a scene. (Cyd Stumpel, winner-verified; the spotlit-installation variant lights each project as an exhibit over a Three.js rig — Iventions, Minh Pham, verified)
- `chapter-world` — chaptered documentary over a continuous nature ground, each chapter peaking and releasing, the axis vertical or horizontal. Route here when the brief supplies chaptered material — a year, an expedition, a season. (OceanX 2025, winner-verified; Sculpting Harmony, media-only)
- `type-index` — density is the argument: no marketing hero, a giant-type index over a living ground, the climax on click. Route here when the volume of work is the pitch. (executable pattern, unverified — no dated winner corroborates the mechanic)

**Reflexes — enumerate, then reject by name before committing.**
1. Deep navy page, three blurred purple-and-teal gradient orbs, one `backdrop-filter` glass card: the AI spatial screenshot.
2. Sage `#87A98F` + terracotta `#C67D5B` + cream lifted unmodified from the archetype's own Color list.
3. `border-radius: 24px` on every surface, offered as the whole argument for "organic".
4. The reflexive circle-follower cursor with magnetic-everything, added before the content has a shape.
5. One `clip-path: ellipse(80% 100% at 50% 0%)` on a single section, rectangles everywhere after it.
6. A Three.js icosahedron rotating under an emissive material, carrying no part of the story.
7. A hero blob SVG that never moves — organic as decoration rather than as substrate.
8. Ambient drift declared as the aliveness answer, with no directed carry down the page and no peak anywhere.

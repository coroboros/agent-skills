# Bold-maximal — tier 1

**Voice.** Density read as choreography. Every viewport inch carries content and every element earns it: layered compositions overlap photography, illustration, 3D and kinetic type under deliberate z-index staging; display faces run 100–300px as art objects, not labels; motion is constant and graded rather than a bank of entrances. The binding law is ONE motion metaphor — one input mapped to one verb — forged in the hero and reused as the page's whole grammar: scroll-is-a-playhead (Ponpon), cursor-distance-drives-type (Exat), section-enter-rotates-color (DICH), one-expo-curve-resolves-every-transition (Cuberto). Visibly different mechanics — button fill, link roll, figure zoom, cursor state — then resolve on one easing family and one color rule. Any mechanic bound to a different primary input is décor, capped at one secondary channel.

**Register licence.** The excess is spent on motion density and channel count, not automatically on color. The color rule is binary: a SATURATED register runs 4–6 hues each holding a fixed role, or one rotated per section (DICH™ Fashion, Awwwards SOTD 2025-06-09 + Developer Award — the rotation rests on this single corpus winner plus Figma); a KINETIC register keeps the palette restrained and lets the type motion carry the maximalism, and most of the corpus lives there — 21 TSI takes Awwwards SOTD 2025-04-12 at 7.38 plus FWA and CSSDA on a strictly monochromatic grade, and Exat scores 8.83 at CSSDA Website of the Month on two hues (`#0000cb → #FF0B00`). Fills resolve to full role colors, never tints. What binds instead: one easing camp (elastic/back OR expo, never both), one full-amplitude mechanic per element class, one peak, and no section running below two live channels.

**Anti-signals — these disqualify.**
- A pale-tint wash on a primary control (`hover:bg-primary/10`, the shadcn leak). The tint is legal only on a ghost or outline variant that starts transparent.
- A nav bar solidifying into an opaque band with a contrasting brand-colored `border-bottom` on scroll — the bar never solidifies at all.
- One universal hover cloned across buttons *and* links *and* cards; winners vary the mechanic by element class and split loud from quiet.
- A uniform fire-once `IntersectionObserver` fade-up on every section: no scrub, no reversal, no graded stagger.
- Kinetic type with no story, the archetype's named failure mode; a lagging-dot cursor with no state, left running on touch.
- A card or bento-grid fold — grids are proof, never the hero. A photographic hero on a type portfolio.
- A decorative `0→100` counter over a curtain resolving into a static hero; a functional-chrome footer.
- Feature-benefit paragraphs and self-narration ("we craft immersive experiences") — the motion argues, the copy labels.

**Macrostructures it runs.**
- `chapter-world` — the cover IS scene one, then chapter-select, operable panel scenes with camera moves and physics, About as the rest. Route here when the brief is a story brand, comic or exhibition the reader operates; requires a WebGL path. (Ponpon Mania, winner-verified)
- `specimen-tour` — self-naming hero, About, operable type-tester, specimen-grid climax mid-page, capability sections quieting, CTA reprise. Route here when the product IS the type or the system and the specimen can perform itself. (Exat, winner-verified)
- `argument-scroll` — declarative pitch hero, featured work as the early climax, services, blog rest, contact reprise; the line's only true funnel. Route here when a studio pitches and the work is the proof. Playground variant when the brief supplies a cast of named subjects instead of a funnel: hero → named or color-temperature chapters → modal/word-reveal CTA → spectacle footer (Warhol Arts SOTD 7.37, DICH Fashion SOTD + Developer Award). (Cuberto, reference-tier; Eloy Benoffi compresses the funnel to one scroll with a closing clone-storm climax, Awwwards HM + GSAP SOTD + CSSDA)

**Reflexes — enumerate, then reject by name before committing.**
1. Six saturated hues thrown at the page with no role assignment: "maximal" read as a palette count.
2. Neon-on-near-black with an animated gradient-mesh ground and a gradient-filled display headline.
3. Clash Display or Satoshi Black at 200px, standing in for a type decision.
4. Density delivered by adding sections and cards until the page feels full, with no channel running underneath them.
5. Reaching for a custom cursor first, before the page has a metaphor for the cursor to express.
6. `mix-blend-mode: difference` sprinkled across the page as the whole art direction.
7. Three spectacles competing — WebGL hero, physics mid-page, particle footer — so no peak reads as the peak.
8. A marquee strip reached for as the ambient channel whenever a section feels quiet.

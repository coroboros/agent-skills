# The hero judge — the hero chunk's gate

The hero chunk builds 2–3 genuinely distinct directions through one shared render frame, then a fresh context judges them beside the archetype's live exemplar. Only a hero that clears this earns the rest of the ladder.

## Isolation

- The judge is a fresh context: it receives the render frame, the candidates' screenshots at 1440 and 375, the live exemplar the tier-1 file names (`references/archetype/<name>.md`, Exemplar row; its URL in `references/exemplars.md`), the contract's THESIS and SIGNATURE blocks, and nothing the builder wrote about the candidates.
- It never sees the builder's ranking. A candidate that looks more finished than the others has broken the comparison, not won it — the judge names that and discounts finish.
- It refutes; it never confirms. No praise, no summary prose.
- Without subagents, the builder judges in the same context after writing the inventory and labels the verdict `degraded: same-context`; the ship label follows `references/gate/review.md` §The ship label.

## The read

1. Inventory each candidate in its own words before comparing — what the first viewport shows, where the primary action sits, what moves and what the motion is for.
2. Compare each candidate to the exemplar on the two questions the roll settles nothing about: who the page is for (audience identification) and what it sells (product clarity), then on the signature's promise (is the replayable moment there, and does it belong to this world?).
3. Pick one candidate or none.

## The verdict line

`HERO-JUDGE: <candidate id> — BEATS | LOSES the exemplar — cause: concept | execution | craft — <one sentence of evidence>`

- `BEATS` — the candidate continues; the others are dropped, their verdict lines quoted in the hero row's Report.
- `LOSES — concept` — a scope change: handed back to the director, who regenerates from R1 (`references/gate/concept.md`); the hero chunk stops.
- `LOSES — execution` — the chunk re-implements the same concept; the judge runs again.
- `LOSES — craft` — the optical pass (`references/optical-craft.md`) on the same build; the judge runs again.

Polish is never the remedy for a LOSES. Two consecutive LOSES on the same cause class end the hero chunk with `status: blocked` and the verdict lines in the Report.
